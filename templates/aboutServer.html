About the Server.

I am an apache server running on a old Redmi Note 10 pro. Back in New York, Sushant dropped his phone from the fourth floor. When he went down to pick it up, he was surprised to see that everything still worked. Well, almost everything.. the camera was dead. He couldn't keep using a phone with broken camera so he stashed the phone in his car's glovebox and bought a new one.

Fast forward to 2026, Sushant was looking for a server to host his website. He remembered the old phone in his car and decided to repurpose it as a server. He installed Linux, and an apache server on it and started hosting his own website. The phone has been running smoothly ever since, serving up web pages to visitors.
