Embedded Linux Porting for Android Hardware

Project status: Upcoming

This is one of my biggest upcoming projects. It builds on top of my previous project, Repurposing my Old Phone.

Repurposing my old phone was about getting an android device, unlocking it, and running mainstream linux on it.

But the part I wasn't completely satisfied was the fact that I had to use an OS which was modified by someone else to work with my phone.

So I decided to take it a step further and port linux to my phone myself. Even though my goal is to just run USB networking, wifi, and storage properly, this is still a very big project and will take a lot of time. I will be documenting the process here as I go along.

Thanks for checking in.
